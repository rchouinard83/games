Hive Bio Lab

Dr. Hive Queen needs a steady hand. Clear the spores before they jam the capsule feed.

How to play

Move and rotate the two-color capsules as they fall. Line up four or more of the same color in a row or column. Spores and capsule halves both count.

Loose halves fall after a clear and can start a chain. Clear every spore to advance. Keep the capsule feed at the top open.

Keyboard: ← / → move, ↓ drops faster, Z / X rotate, and P pauses. On a phone, tap the play screen to rotate clockwise, swipe left or right to move, or swipe down to drop faster. You can also use the buttons under the chamber.
