Grid Siege: alien invasion attackers

The invasion escalates as the waves go on. It starts with simple aliens, then scary ones, then their craft, simple first and advanced last. Every attacker keeps its color across all four stages (on the craft it becomes the lights and glow), so you can still tell them apart at a glance. Under each design is a lane at real game size with its HP bar.
